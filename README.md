# SS Holidays – Content Marketing Project

## 📌 Project Overview

This project showcases a **4-week social media content marketing campaign** created for **SS Holidays**, a travel and tourism brand targeting college students.

The campaign focused on promoting affordable, student-friendly travel packages through **Instagram and Facebook**, with content designed to increase engagement, reach, followers, and package enquiries.

## 🎯 Campaign Objectives

- Increase social media awareness for SS Holidays
- Grow Instagram followers
- Increase Facebook reach
- Improve audience engagement
- Generate enquiries for travel packages
- Build trust through useful travel content

## 📱 Content Strategy

The campaign was built around four major content pillars:

### 1. 🌄 Inspiration
Destination reels and travel visuals designed to capture students' attention and inspire them to travel.

### 2. 🎓 Education
Travel tips, packing guides, FAQs, and useful information to provide value to the audience.

### 3. 📢 Promotion
Travel package highlights, student offers, and limited-seat promotions designed to generate enquiries.

### 4. 💬 Engagement
Polls, questions, and relatable content designed to encourage comments, shares, and interaction.

## 📅 Campaign Schedule

The campaign ran for **4 weeks**, with **3 posts per week**, resulting in **12 posts**.

Content included:

- Ooty Destination Reel
- Kodaikanal Destination Reel
- Munnar Destination Reel
- Travel Tips
- Packing Guide Carousel
- Package Promotion Posts
- Student Offers
- Polls
- Engagement Questions
- Travel Recap Content

## #️⃣ Hashtag Strategy

Hashtags were divided into three categories:

- **High Volume:** #Travel #CollegeLife #StudentLife #WeekendTrip
- **Medium Volume:** #TravelTamilNadu #CollegeTrip #BudgetTravel #TripWithFriends
- **Niche / Local:** #SSHolidays #KECStudents #ErodeTravel #KEC #OotyPackage

The strategy focused on combining broad-reach hashtags with location- and audience-specific hashtags.
